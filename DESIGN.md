# SiMiTsu visual direction

## Identity

SiMiTsu is a public tsunami education experience with a scientific geospatial interface. Keep the existing SiMiTsu name and wave mark. The product should feel calm, legible, geographically grounded, and useful to the public, not like an operational alert console.

## Audience and mood

Write for people learning about tsunami causes, historical events, preparedness, and educational scenarios. Use precise language for data and model limits. Use plain, editorial language for education and mitigation.

## Palette

- Deep ocean navy is the fixed initial theme and the ground for map and simulation work.
- Clear blue identifies water, navigation focus, and primary actions.
- Warm orange is reserved for selected emphasis and caution.
- Risk colors keep their established meanings and appear only with an explicit text label.
- Geographic line colors distinguish map features, not risk levels.

## Typography and composition

Use the system sans-serif stack for fast loading and familiar reading. Give headings, body copy, map labels, and model values distinct sizes and weights. Use tabular numerals for comparable measurements.

Let the map and simulator occupy the visual area their tasks need. Keep explanatory copy in editorial text and lists rather than boxing every item into a card. Vary section composition according to whether the section teaches, compares records, or supports exploration.

## Motion and motif

Retain the existing wave mark as the product motif. Use motion only to explain tsunami processes or respond to direct user control. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid ambient decorative animation.

## Dials

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1

Reading this as: a tsunami education homepage for the Indonesian public, in a scientific geospatial interface, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

## Decision reasons

- The dark ocean palette preserves SiMiTsu's established identity and keeps scientific map and simulator surfaces visually coherent.
- Blue marks water and primary interface focus; orange marks caution and selective emphasis; risk colors remain reserved for explicitly labeled risk categories.
- The system sans-serif keeps the static site readable without adding a remote font dependency.
- The home composition pairs the headline and actions with the existing Leaflet map because geographic context is the product's main entry point.
- Editorial sections use headings, paragraphs, and lists so maps, records, and explanations do not all become interchangeable cards.
- Low-motion presentation keeps attention on the data; existing simulations and animations remain available for intentional interaction.
